Moving a hardware R&D team from waterfall to Scrum

Hardware doesn't sprint like software: a board takes weeks to arrive and a cleanroom run can't be split into two-day tickets. Still, moving our pre-development team from waterfall to Scrum helped.

What we changed

  • Sprints around learning, not parts. Each sprint ends with a decision or a measurement, even while hardware is in the mail.
  • One shared view. Azure DevOps, Kanban boards and Smartsheet gave simulation, firmware, testing and procurement the same picture.
  • Retrospectives per prototype. Lessons from each phase flow into the next design.

What stayed waterfall

Budgets, supplier lead times and the design verification plan still need fixed milestones. Scrum runs inside those gates, not instead of them.

The result

Booked hours and cost fell by 10% for the same output compared with the year before. That is a team effect more than a framework effect, but the framework made waste visible.

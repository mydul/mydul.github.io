From cleanroom to project plan

I started in cleanrooms and labs, ran a production shift, and now plan R&D projects. What carried over?

1. Root-cause thinking

On the shift I brought defects from about 40% to about 10% by finding causes instead of treating symptoms. A risk register is the same habit, applied before things break.

2. Respect for process steps

A lithography step has a recipe and a tolerance. Projects have them too: scope, budget, timeline.

3. Talking to specialists

Knowing what a sputtering run or a verification test involves lets me ask better questions and spot unrealistic estimates early.

What I had to learn

Stakeholders, budgets and saying no. A physics lab doesn't teach those.

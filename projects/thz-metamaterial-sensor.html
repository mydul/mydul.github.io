M.Sc. thesis · University of Siegen

THz metamaterial microfluidic biosensor

Problem

Biosensing needs a sensitive, label-free readout. THz waves are sensitive to liquids, but water absorbs them strongly, so the liquid volume must be tiny and well controlled.

Approach

  • Fabricated metamaterial sensors in the cleanroom: photolithography, plasma/RIE etching and lift-off, plus CVD/PVD deposition.
  • Built the microfluidic layers by soft imprinting with PDMS and TMCS surface treatment.
  • Checked quality with optical microscopy and profilometry; measured with THz frequency-domain spectroscopy.

Result

Working chips measured at THz frequencies. The supervisor rated the thesis Very Good and noted that the soft-imprint approach was developed independently.

Survey on Electrical Design Automation Tools and Software

A guided comparison of free/open-source and proprietary EDA tools.

🧾Abstract

The paper surveys the software tools used in electronic design automation (EDA), across operating systems and platforms. It sorts the tools by the task they serve and then compares the two development models, free/open-source and proprietary software, on the features that matter when choosing a tool.

🚪Introduction

Integrated circuits keep growing in complexity at an exponential rate. Early geometric design software could not keep to the layer limits of complex circuits, so performance dropped. EDA tools were built to solve this. They cover design from system level down to layout, with their own synthesis, verification and analysis tool sets, and many companies keep extending them.

⚠️Problem

There are many EDA tools, and they differ a lot. Free tools rarely cover every step, so users combine several of them. Proprietary suites cover more but come with licences and cost. Engineers and students need a clear picture of what each kind can and cannot do before they pick one.

✨What's new

  • One overview that groups the tools by function and by licence type.
  • A side-by-side comparison of open-source and proprietary tools on seven criteria.
  • Practical notes on which free tool fits which task, for example synthesis, physical layout or FPGA work.

🧭Approach

Tools were first classified by function (schematic entry, PCB design, simulation, Gerber viewing and so on) and then split into two licence groups. The free group was divided into three areas:

🔬Evaluation

This is a survey, so there was no lab experiment. Each tool family was described by its purpose and typical use. The two licence models were then compared on development, support, flexibility, cost, features, accuracy and efficiency.

📊Findings

CriterionProprietaryOpen source / free
DevelopmentHandled by the vendor, at its discretion.Community collaboration, lasts while the community is active.
SupportManuals, FAQs and a contact point.Limited, often no organised support.
FlexibilityAs much as the vendor allows.Can be modified and extended.
CostLicence fee.No cost.
FeaturesMaximum features and a polished interface.No single tool offers everything.
AccuracyUsually higher.Not always fully accurate.
EfficiencyConsistently better.Average.

✅Summary

  • EDA tools split into two licence groups: proprietary and free/open-source.
  • Proprietary suites (Synopsys, Cadence, Mentor Graphics) lead on features, accuracy, efficiency and support.
  • Free tools cost nothing and can be modified, but you often need several of them to cover a full design flow.
  • The best choice depends on the user's needs, budget and how much support and accuracy the project requires.

THz metamaterial microfluidic biosensor chip

A label-free sensor chip for tiny liquid samples, fabricated and measured at THz frequencies.

⌄
Where
University of SiegenInstitute of High Frequency and Quantum Electronics
When
Completed May 2023
Tools
PhotolithographyRIELift-offCVD / PVDPDMSTHz-TDS

⚠️Problem

Biosensing needs a sensitive, label-free readout. THz waves react strongly to liquids, but water absorbs them heavily, so the liquid volume must be tiny and well controlled.

🧭Approach

  • Fabricated metamaterial sensors in the cleanroom with photolithography, plasma/RIE etching, lift-off, and CVD/PVD deposition.
  • Built the microfluidic layer by soft imprinting with PDMS and TMCS surface treatment.
  • Checked quality with optical microscopy and profilometry, and measured with THz frequency-domain spectroscopy.

✅Summary

  • Working chips were fabricated and measured at THz frequencies.
  • The soft-imprint approach was developed independently.
  • The thesis was graded Very Good.

Memristor: window functions and logic gates

A comparison of memristor window functions and hybrid CMOS/memristor logic gates.

⌄
Where

Tools

🧭Approach

  • Compared window functions that model the memristor's boundary behaviour.
  • Designed logic gates in memristor ratioed logic (MRL) style.

✅Summary

Lab project · University of Siegen

Graphene field-effect transistor

Problem

Graphene transistor performance depends heavily on fabrication quality, so each step has to be controlled and then verified by measurement.

Approach

  • Grew graphene by chemical vapour deposition.
  • Patterned devices with optical lithography.
  • Deposited contacts by evaporation (PVD).
  • Measured carrier mobility and transconductance.

Result

Working GFET devices, characterised for mobility and transconductance.
